I study how people think about, rely on, and resist artificial intelligence, and build validated instruments to measure it.

About

I am a Research Engineer (Senior) at the Visual Cognitive Neuroscience Lab, School of Social Sciences, Nanyang Technological University. My research sits at the intersection of psychology and human–AI interaction: I develop and validate psychometric scales for constructs such as critical thinking in AI use, AI fatigue, AI usability and obedience to AI, and I use computational and AI/ML methods to study trust, media credibility and well-being.

I hold an M.Soc.Sci. in Psychology and a B.Soc.Sci. (Honours with Highest Distinction) from the National University of Singapore, completed through its Concurrent Degree Programme, with summer exchanges at Harvard University and Yale University. I have led multiple research projects, including international collaborations, and in 2023–2025 I received the university's highest performance rating for academic research staff.

I serve on the Editorial Board of Technology, Mind, and Behavior and review for journals including Computers & Education: Artificial Intelligence, International Journal of Human-Computer Interaction, IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing and the Journal of Medical Internet Research.
